Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the platform.

How does the AI-powered roadmap generation work?

Our system uses advanced AI to analyze your current skill level and learning goals. It then curates a personalized learning path with specific resources, videos, and projects tailored just for you.

Can I track my progress and collaborate with my team?

Yes! TechPath allows you to track completed modules and collaborate with team members on shared projects. You can monitor team progress through the dashboard.

How is my data security and privacy handled?

We take data security seriously. All personal information and progress data are encrypted both in transit and at rest, fully compliant with modern data protection standards.

Are there different account tiers or subscription details?

TechPath offers a free tier with basic resources and an advanced tier that unlocks premium AI features and team collaboration tools.
